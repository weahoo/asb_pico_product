01 / 03
AI / MCP

StackHub

Controlled AI tools for computers that stay off the internet

Bring authorized AI tools to a computer that does not connect directly to the internet. StackHub uses its own network and a USB Bridge to reach selected local MCP tools.

  • The target computer can stay off the internet and Wi-Fi
  • Controlled access to configured local tools and data
  • MCP-compatible AI and supported StackChan; Siri / Shortcuts planned

Hardware is sold separately. USB still exchanges data; this is not a zero-exchange air gap.

Use your phone, tablet or an older device as a keyboard, trackpad, shortcut panel and lightweight pressure pen for your desktop.

  • Use a browser for everyday control—no extra app needed
  • USB HID and supported Bluetooth modes
  • Handy for shortcuts, presentations, signatures and desktop tasks

Capabilities depend on host support and connection mode.

TransferHub
