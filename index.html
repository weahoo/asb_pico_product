Two ways to connect

Plug it in, or go wireless.

SmartHub talks to the device you want to control in whichever way suits you. Either way, your phone, tablet or computer is the one in your hand.

With a cable

Plug straight in (USB)

Connect SmartHub to the computer, screen or TV box you want to control with a USB data cable. It's recognised right away as a mouse and keyboard — nothing to install, nothing to pair.

Great for: your desk computer, a work laptop, anything with a free USB port.

Wireless

Pair over Bluetooth

Just give SmartHub power — a phone charger or power bank is perfect. After a few seconds, open the Bluetooth settings on the device you want to control and pick “visualbuild-ble”. Done!

Great for: tablets, TV boxes like Apple TV, and devices without a spare USB port.

SmartHub chooses for you

When it powers up, SmartHub checks: connected to a device by cable? It works over USB. Only getting power? It switches to Bluetooth. Want to change? Just unplug it and plug it back in the other way.

FAQ

Good questions, quick answers

Do I need to download an app?

Not for everyday use — point, type and draw without installing anything. You'll only need our app when you want to update SmartHub to the latest version, and that takes just a few taps.

Which devices work?

Phones, tablets and computers — almost anything with a web browser, old or new. Even a phone that no longer gets updates works great.

Can I really draw with it?

Yes! Use your finger or a stylus on a phone or tablet, and your strokes show up on the bigger screen — great for sketches, doodles, handwritten notes and signatures.

Can I switch between mouse, keyboard and drawing?

Anytime. Move the pointer, type a few words, then sketch — all from the same device, with a single tap to switch.

Do I need to install anything on the device I control?

Nope. Whether you plug in with a USB cable or pair over Bluetooth, SmartHub shows up as an ordinary mouse and keyboard. An app only comes in on the occasional day you update SmartHub.

USB or Bluetooth — which should I choose?

Whatever is easiest! Got a free USB port? Plug in and you're done. No port, or controlling a tablet or TV box? Power SmartHub from a charger and pair it over Bluetooth. Tip: pair it on the device you want to control — not on the phone in your hand.

Is it a remote control?

Only as a little extra. SmartHub is made for pointing, typing and drawing — but if your usual remote goes missing, your phone can help out in a pinch.

Will it change anything on my device?

No. Your photos, messages and files stay exactly as they are. SmartHub simply gives your device one more way to be useful.
