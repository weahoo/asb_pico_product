Two ways to connect

Plug it in, or go wireless.

Flexhub talks to the device you want to control in whichever way suits you. Either way, your phone, tablet or computer is the one in your hand.

With a cable

Plug straight in (USB)

Connect Flexhub to the computer, screen or TV box you want to control with a USB data cable. It's recognised right away as a mouse and keyboard — nothing to install, nothing to pair.

Great for: your desk computer, a work laptop, anything with a free USB port.

Wireless

Pair over Bluetooth

Just give Flexhub power — a phone charger or power bank is perfect. After a few seconds, open the Bluetooth settings on the device you want to control and pick “flexhub”. Done!

Great for: tablets, TV boxes like Apple TV, and devices without a spare USB port.

USB vs Bluetooth — at a glance

Same sunny control from your phone either way. Here's what each mode brings.

What you get USB mode Bluetooth mode
How it connects USB data cable into the device you want to control Power only, then pair flexhub on that device

Gamepad (DualSense & classic BT) ✓ Yes — DualSense & classic Bluetooth pads – No gamepad in Bluetooth mode

Flexhub chooses for you

When it powers up, Flexhub checks: connected to a device by cable? It works over USB. Only getting power? It switches to Bluetooth. Want to change? Just unplug it and plug it back in the other way.

FAQ

Good questions, quick answers

Do I need to download an app?

Not for everyday use — shortcuts, pointing, typing and light annotate without installing anything. You’ll only need our app when you want to update Flexhub, and that takes a few taps.

How do I connect over Wi‑Fi?

On your phone, tablet or computer, join the Wi‑Fi named flexhub (password build.me), then open http://flexhub.local/ or http://192.168.4.1/ in the browser. That wireless hotspot is how Flexhub talks to the device in your hand — no App Store install for everyday use.

Which devices work?

Phones, tablets and computers — almost anything with a web browser, old or new. Even a phone that no longer gets updates works great as a wireless shortcut panel — for example, a first-gen Apple iPad mini.

Can I use it for drawing?

For light, occasional use — yes: PDF annotate, contract signatures, kids’ doodles and beginner doodles. No pressure levels — not a Wacom tablet.

Can I switch between mouse, keyboard and drawing?

Anytime. Fire a shortcut, move the pointer, type a few words, then annotate — all from the same device, with a single tap to switch.

Do I need to install anything on the device I control?

Nope. Whether you plug in with a USB cable or pair over Bluetooth, Flexhub shows up as an ordinary mouse and keyboard. An app only comes in on the occasional day you update Flexhub.

USB or Bluetooth — which should I choose?

Whatever is easiest! Got a free USB port? Plug in and you're done. No port, or controlling a tablet or TV box? Power Flexhub from a charger and pair it over Bluetooth. Tip: pair it on the device you want to control — not on the phone in your hand.

Is it a remote control?

Only as a little extra. Flexhub is made for shortcuts, pointing and light touch — but if your usual remote goes missing, your phone can help in a pinch.

Will it change anything on my device?

No. Your photos, messages and files stay exactly as they are. Flexhub simply gives your device one more way to be useful.

Can it replace my Wacom tablet?

No — and we say that up front. Flexhub has no pressure levels. It shines as a light trackpad, wireless presenter, signature pad, multimedia shortcut controller, and macros from key combos for Stream Deck–like shortcuts.

Can I set my own shortcut keys and macros?

Yes. On the Flexhub Wi‑Fi page you can record macros, save them to the device, and replay them later as shortcut-like actions.
