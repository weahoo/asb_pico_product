FAQ

Good questions, quick answers

Which old phones and tablets work?

Almost any phone or tablet with a web browser — even ones that no longer get updates. No app download needed.

Will it change anything on my old phone?

No. Your photos, messages and memories stay exactly as they are. SmartHub simply gives the phone a new way to be useful.

Can I use a game controller too?

Yes! Pair your wireless controller and play from the sofa — no cable running across the room.

Do I need to install anything on my computer?

Nope. Just plug SmartHub in and it's ready to go.

Does it replace my other remotes?

Think of it as a trusty backup. Keep your usual remote — and when it goes missing or runs out of battery, SmartHub has you covered.
