This policy covers VisualBuild, its website and the StackHub, FlexHub and TransferHub products. It explains the information used when you browse, order, request support or enable optional online services.
## 1. Operator and privacy contact
VisualBuild is independently operated. For privacy requests or information about the seller, email [info@visualbuild.me](mailto:info@visualbuild.me). Additional seller details can be requested through our [contact page](contact.html). Information required to be disclosed publicly by applicable law remains subject to those requirements.
## 2. Visiting the website
Our website is hosted on Vercel. Hosting systems may process IP addresses, browser data, pages requested and security logs to deliver and protect the site. The pages also load Google Fonts, which may connect your browser to Google's servers.
Your language or guide preferences may be stored locally in your browser. We do not sell customer information or use it for behavioural advertising.
## 3. Using our products
FlexHub and TransferHub support local device functions; settings, recordings or files may remain on your own devices or browser according to the function used. Protect local Wi-Fi access and delete private data before selling or returning hardware.
StackHub can use an online gateway for activation, device connections, service access and subscriptions. Technical information such as device identifiers, connection status, recovery-code verification data, service entitlements and usage records may be processed. MCP tool requests and responses may pass between your chosen AI provider, the gateway and the tools you connect. Do not submit secrets unless you understand the services and permissions involved.
Your third-party AI account, API keys (BYOK) and any connected external service are also governed by that provider's privacy terms.
## 4. Orders, payment and support
If you buy a product or contact us, we may process your email, name, delivery details, order and payment status, support messages and attachments as needed for the purchase or request. Payment details are handled by the payment provider shown at checkout; we do not need your full card number. Delivery details may be shared with the carrier when hardware is shipped.
## 5. Why and with whom we process data
We process information to provide requested services, fulfil orders, answer enquiries, protect service security and meet legal requirements. Where applicable, legal bases include performance of a contract, legitimate interests and legal obligations. Necessary recipients may include hosting, email, payment and delivery providers, the StackHub gateway and AI services you choose. Some providers may process data internationally under their own terms and applicable safeguards.
## 6. Storage and protection
Information is kept only as needed for the purpose, ongoing service, dispute resolution and applicable accounting or legal duties. Browser or device-local records remain until you clear them or reset the device. We use reasonable access controls and secure connections where available, but no system is completely risk-free.
## 7. Your choices and rights
Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection to certain processing, and complain to a competent regulator. Email [info@visualbuild.me](mailto:info@visualbuild.me) for a request. We may verify ownership before disclosing or changing records. This policy may be updated when products or processing change.
