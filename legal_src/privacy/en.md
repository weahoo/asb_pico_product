## 3. Data we process on the Website

When you open a page, your browser automatically sends technical data to the hosting servers, such as your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested, the referring page, and your browser and operating system (user agent). Legal basis: our legitimate interest in providing a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### 3.2 Fonts from Google Fonts
To display text in all supported languages, the Website loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and Google LLC, USA ("Google"). To deliver the fonts, your browser connects directly to Google's servers, which receive your IP address, the requested page address (referrer) and your user agent. According to Google, Google Fonts does not set cookies and the data is not used to build user profiles. More information: https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a consistent, readable presentation of the Website in all languages (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If you prefer not to connect to Google, you can block fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com in your browser; the Website will then use your system fonts.

### 3.3 Local storage in your browser
The Website does not use cookies. It saves two small values in your browser's local storage so that your choices are remembered:
- flexhub_lang: the language you selected.
- flexhub_guide_mode: the connection mode (USB or wireless) you last viewed in the user guide.

These values never leave your device and are not sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete them at any time by clearing your browser's site data. Because they are strictly necessary to provide the function you requested, no consent is required for them.

## 4. Data processed by the Flexhub device
Flexhub is a small hardware device based on the Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W. It creates a local Wi‑Fi hotspot (network name "flexhub") and serves a control page directly from the device. It then acts as a keyboard, mouse and similar input device for the computer, tablet or TV it is connected to, by USB or Bluetooth.
- No account and no cloud: the device does not connect to the internet and does not transmit any data to us or to third parties.
- Local storage: settings, recorded macros, the names of buttons you configure and Bluetooth pairing information are stored only in the device's internal memory.
- Inputs: what you tap, type or draw on the control page is passed on in real time to the connected device as keyboard, mouse or pen input. It is not recorded, except for macros you choose to record and save.
- Nearby devices: while the hotspot is on, other people within Wi‑Fi range who know the hotspot password could join it. Unplug Flexhub when you are not using it, and do not record passwords or other secrets as macros, because macros are stored on the device and may not be encrypted.
- Deleting data: you can delete saved macros and settings on the control page or by resetting the device as described in the [user guide](guide.html). Please reset the device before you give it away, sell it or return it.

Because this data stays on your device and we never receive it, we are not the controller of it.
