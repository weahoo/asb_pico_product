This policy explains how you can return a Flexhub device bought on visualbuild.shop and how refunds work. It is part of our [Terms of Sale and Use](terms.html). It does not limit your statutory rights.

## 1. 30-day return policy
In addition to any statutory rights, you can return a Product for any reason within 30 days of delivery and receive a refund of the purchase price.

To be eligible:
- the Product must be complete, with all accessories and, if possible, the original packaging;
- the Product must not be damaged beyond what is needed to check its nature, characteristics and functioning;
- please reset the device before returning it, so that your settings and macros are deleted.

The withdrawal period will expire after 14 days from the day on which you acquire, or a third party other than the carrier and indicated by you acquires, physical possession of the goods. If you ordered several goods in one order that are delivered separately, the period expires 14 days after you or your nominee receives the last item.

To exercise the right of withdrawal, you must inform us ([Company name], [Registered address], email: [Contact email]) of your decision to withdraw from this contract by an unequivocal statement (for example, a letter sent by post or an email). You may use the model withdrawal form below, but it is not obligatory. If you withdraw from this contract, we shall reimburse to you all payments received from you, including the costs of delivery (with the exception of the supplementary costs resulting from your choice of a type of delivery other than the least expensive type of standard delivery offered by us), without undue delay and in any event not later than 14 days from the day on which we are informed about your decision to withdraw from this contract. You are only liable for any diminished value of the goods resulting from handling other than what is necessary to establish the nature, characteristics and functioning of the goods.

## 3. How to return a Product
- Email [Contact email] with your order number and the reason for the return (optional for withdrawals).
- We will reply with the return address and instructions.
- Pack the Product securely and send it back. We recommend a tracked shipping service and keeping proof of postage.

## 4. Defective or dead-on-arrival devices
If your Flexhub does not work on arrival or develops a defect, please contact us at [Contact email] as soon as possible with your order number, a description of the problem and, if possible, a photo or video. Many issues can be solved with the troubleshooting section of the [user guide](guide.html), so we may first suggest a few checks.

If the device is defective, you are entitled to the remedies under your statutory rights and our limited warranty: repair, replacement or a refund. For devices that are defective on arrival or covered by the warranty, we pay the return shipping costs and the cost of sending a repaired or replacement device.

## 5. Shipping costs
- Withdrawals and 30-day returns for reasons other than a defect: you pay the cost of returning the Product. If you return the whole order, we refund the original standard delivery costs.
- Defective or wrong items: we pay all shipping costs.
- Import duties and taxes paid by you for the original delivery are refunded where we can recover them or where the law requires it.

## 6. Exceptions
- Products damaged by misuse, accidents, liquids or improper power supplies, or modified or opened by anyone other than us, are not eligible for the voluntary 30-day return; your statutory rights remain unaffected.
- Business customers have no statutory right of withdrawal; the voluntary 30-day return policy applies to them as well.

## 7. Cancelling a pre-order
You can cancel a pre-order at any time before it ships by emailing [Contact email]. We will refund the full amount you paid.

## 8. Refunds
We refund to the original payment method within 14 days of receiving the returned Product (or proof that you sent it, for withdrawals). We will confirm the refund by email. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take a few additional business days for the amount to appear in your account.
