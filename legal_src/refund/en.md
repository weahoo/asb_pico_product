This policy covers hardware bought directly from VisualBuild: StackHub, FlexHub and TransferHub. Statutory consumer rights always apply. For help, email [info@visualbuild.me](mailto:info@visualbuild.me).
## 1. Voluntary 30-day hardware returns
For eligible FlexHub purchases, the previously published voluntary return offer permits a return request within 30 days after delivery. Other hardware is included only if its purchase terms explicitly offer this benefit. Return the complete product with accessories and, where available, its original packaging, without damage beyond reasonable inspection. Reset the device and remove private settings before returning it. This offer is additional to statutory rights.
## 2. Statutory withdrawal for eligible consumers
Consumers in the EU/EEA and UK generally have a statutory 14-day withdrawal right for qualifying online purchases of goods, starting when they (or their nominee) receive the goods; for items delivered separately, the final delivery matters. To withdraw, send an unequivocal notice to [info@visualbuild.me](mailto:info@visualbuild.me) before the period ends. No reason is needed. Exceptions provided by applicable law may apply.
## 3. Return procedure
Email us your order number and product name, stating whether it is a withdrawal, voluntary return or defect claim. We will provide the appropriate return address and instructions by email; please do not send items before receiving them. For a statutory withdrawal, return the goods without undue delay, normally within 14 days after notifying us. Keep proof of dispatch.
## 4. Refunds and shipping charges
For a valid statutory withdrawal, we refund the eligible payments and original standard delivery charges as required by law, normally within 14 days after receiving your withdrawal notice; we may wait until the goods are returned or you provide proof of sending them. Under the voluntary 30-day offer, eligible refunds are made following receipt and inspection of the goods, generally within 14 days. Refunds normally use the original payment method. For change-of-mind returns, you may bear direct return postage where law permits and you were informed in advance. For defective or incorrectly supplied items, legally required return costs are our responsibility.
## 5. Defective products
If a product arrives damaged, does not work or does not match its description, contact us with the order details and, if possible, photos or a description. Applicable law may provide free repair, replacement, price reduction or refund. No voluntary-return condition limits those rights.
## 6. Pre-orders and digital services
An accepted hardware pre-order may be cancelled before dispatch by emailing us, with payment returned according to the agreed terms and law. The 30-day hardware return offer does not automatically apply to StackHub digital plans or subscriptions. Digital cancellations, renewals, refunds and any statutory withdrawal rights follow applicable law and the conditions disclosed at checkout; no automatic waiver of rights is assumed.
## 7. Example withdrawal notice
To withdraw, email a clear statement such as “I wish to withdraw from my order.” Include the product name, order number, order date and delivery date where known. Request seller or return-address details at [info@visualbuild.me](mailto:info@visualbuild.me).
