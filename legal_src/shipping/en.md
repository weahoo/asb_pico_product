This policy concerns physical StackHub, FlexHub and TransferHub orders. Digital StackHub services and subscriptions have no physical delivery. For a shipping question, email [info@visualbuild.me](mailto:info@visualbuild.me).
## 1. Delivery destinations
Available countries and regions depend on the product and delivery arrangements. Check the destination options during checkout or ask us before ordering. An unavailable destination is not a promise of future service.
## 2. Processing and delivery time
Dispatch and delivery estimates are provided with the product offer, at checkout or in the accepted order information when available. We do not publish an unverified universal dispatch period or arrival date. If a delivery estimate is missing, ask us before paying. Items marked as pre-orders may ship later; review their stated timing before ordering.
## 3. Costs, duties and taxes
Shipping charges and any taxes included in the checkout total should be reviewed before payment. Depending on the destination, import duties, customs fees or local taxes may arise separately. Whether such fees are prepaid or payable on import depends on the shipping arrangement shown for your order; please ask us before ordering if unclear.
## 4. Delivery and tracking
Provide a complete delivery address and contact us promptly if it needs correction. We will share dispatch or tracking details when available through the carrier or order process. Carriers and tracking services may vary by destination; no specific carrier is guaranteed unless stated for your order.
## 5. Late, lost or damaged packages
If a parcel is delayed, missing or damaged, email [info@visualbuild.me](mailto:info@visualbuild.me) with the product, order number and, if possible, photos. We will investigate and provide the repair, replacement or refund remedies required by applicable law. Mandatory consumer delivery-risk protections remain unchanged.
## 6. Returns and seller details
For return instructions and a privately communicated return address, read our [Refund and Return Policy](refund.html) and contact us before posting goods. For seller information required by law, use our [contact page](contact.html). We do not publish unverified postal addresses, carriers or shipping schedules.
