Two ways to use Flexhub.
Pick yours — the guide follows.Flexhub 有两种用法。
选好一种，整页跟着切换。

Every time Flexhub powers up it settles into one of two modes, automatically. Choose the one that matches your setup and every section below — steps, timing, tips and troubleshooting — switches to it.Flexhub 每次通电都会自动进入两种模式之一。选择跟你的接法一致的那一种，下面所有内容——步骤、等待时间、小贴士和常见问题——都会跟着切换。

Same in both modes: you hold a phone, tablet or second computer, join Flexhub’s own Wi‑Fi hotspot flexhub and control everything from a web page — no app for everyday use.两种模式都一样：你手里拿手机、平板或另一台电脑，连上 Flexhub 自己的 Wi‑Fi 热点 flexhub，在网页里操作——日常使用不需要 App。

From the box to control, step by step.从拆封到上手，一步一步来。

You need: Flexhub, a USB data cable, the computer to control, and a phone, tablet or second computer with a web browser.准备：Flexhub、一根 USB 数据线、要控制的电脑，以及一台带浏览器的手机、平板或另一台电脑。

You need: Flexhub, a USB charger or power bank with a cable, the device to control (with Bluetooth), and a phone, tablet or computer with a web browser.准备：Flexhub、USB 充电头或充电宝及线、要控制的设备（需有蓝牙），以及一台带浏览器的手机、平板或电脑。

USB modeUSB 模式

  1. 1Plug Flexhub into the computer把 Flexhub 插到电脑上

    Use a USB data cable — charge-only cables don’t carry data. Nothing to install: the computer sees a standard keyboard and mouse named Flexhub (Windows, macOS, Linux and other systems that accept a USB keyboard and mouse).一定要用 USB 数据线——只能充电的线传不了数据。不用装任何东西：电脑会看到一个名为 Flexhub 的标准键盘和鼠标（Windows、macOS、Linux 等支持 USB 键鼠的系统都行）。

  2. 2Join the flexhub Wi‑Fi连上 flexhub Wi‑Fi

    On the phone, tablet or computer in your hand, connect to Wi‑Fi flexhub, password build.me. The hotspot starts as soon as Flexhub has power. Your phone may say “no internet” — that’s expected, stay connected.在你手里的手机、平板或电脑上连接 Wi‑Fi flexhub，密码 build.me。Flexhub 一通电热点就会开启。手机可能提示「无法上网」——这是正常的，保持连接即可。

  3. 3Open the Flexhub page打开 Flexhub 网页

    Many phones pop up a “sign in to network” sheet that opens Flexhub by itself. If not, open the browser and go to http://192.168.4.1/. http://flexhub.local/ works on many devices too — give it about 10 seconds after joining, and use the number address if the name won’t load.很多手机会自动弹出「登录网络」页，直接打开 Flexhub。如果没有，就在浏览器里输入 http://192.168.4.1/。http://flexhub.local/ 在很多设备上也能用——连上后等约 10 秒；名字打不开就用数字地址。

  4. 4Sign in and pick a language登录并选择语言

    Choose English or 中文 at the top of the sign-in page, then enter the access password — it starts as build.me. Change it any time in Settings → Security. (The Wi‑Fi password gets you onto the hotspot; this one unlocks control.)在登录页顶部选择 English 或中文，然后输入访问密码——初始为 build.me，之后可在「设置 → 安全」里修改。（Wi‑Fi 密码用来连热点，这个密码用来解锁控制。）

  5. 5Check “Ready” and start看到「就绪」就开始

    The status at the top of Remote control should read Ready. Detecting connection means Flexhub is still deciding (first seconds after power-up); Connect target device means the computer isn’t seen — check the cable. Then pick a tab: Mouse, Creative Pad, Keyboard, Presenter…「遥控」页顶部的状态应显示就绪。显示正在检测连接说明 Flexhub 还在判断（通电后最初几秒）；显示请连接目标设备说明没识别到电脑——检查数据线。然后选择标签：鼠标、创意画板、键盘、演示器……

Wireless mode无线模式

  1. 1Give Flexhub power only只给 Flexhub 供电

    Plug it into a USB phone charger or power bank — not into a computer (a computer data connection puts it in USB mode). About 10–15 seconds after power-up, with no computer answering, Flexhub settles into wireless mode.插在 USB 手机充电头或充电宝上——不要插电脑（和电脑有数据连接就会进入 USB 模式）。通电约 10–15 秒后，没有电脑应答，Flexhub 就进入无线模式。

  2. 2Join the Wi‑Fi and sign in (while you wait)连 Wi‑Fi 并登录（边等边做）

    On the phone in your hand: Wi‑Fi flexhub, password build.me. Open the pop-up sign-in sheet or http://192.168.4.1/ (or http://flexhub.local/ after ~10 s). Pick English / 中文 and sign in with the access password — build.me until you change it.在你手里的手机上：连 Wi‑Fi flexhub，密码 build.me。打开自动弹出的登录页，或输入 http://192.168.4.1/（约 10 秒后也可用 http://flexhub.local/）。选择 English / 中文，用访问密码登录——未修改前是 build.me。

  3. 3Pair “flexhub” on the device you want to control在要控制的设备上配对「flexhub」

    Bluetooth starts about 2 minutes after power-up (it waits so phones can join the hotspot first). Then open Bluetooth settings on the tablet, computer or TV box — not on the phone in your hand — and choose flexhub. Exact menu paths are in the picker below.蓝牙在通电约 2 分钟后启动（先让手机连上热点）。之后在平板、电脑或电视盒子的蓝牙设置里——不是你手里的手机——选择 flexhub。各系统的具体菜单见下方选择器。

  4. 4Check the status确认状态

    On Remote control, Ready means a Bluetooth target is connected. Connect target device (“pair in target Bluetooth settings”) means nothing is paired yet. The page can’t see what a TV does — always check the result on the target screen.在「遥控」页，就绪表示蓝牙目标已连接；请连接目标设备（“请在目标设备蓝牙设置中配对”）表示还没配对。网页看不到电视的实际反应——以目标屏幕上的结果为准。

  5. 5Control it开始操作

    Use Mouse, Keyboard, Creative Pad and Presenter for tablets and computers; use the Apple TV or Google TV tab for TV boxes (Google TV tab also suits Xiaomi and other Android TV).平板和电脑用鼠标、键盘、创意画板、演示器；电视盒子用 Apple TV 或 Google TV 标签（Google TV 标签也适用于小米等 Android TV）。

What Flexhub does at power-upFlexhub 通电后会做什么
  1. 0 sHotspot on热点开启

    The flexhub Wi‑Fi starts right away.flexhub Wi‑Fi 立刻开启。

  2. ~5 sComputer found → USB mode发现电脑 → USB 模式

    The computer accepts the Flexhub keyboard + mouse. Locked in until the next power-up.电脑接受 Flexhub 键盘 + 鼠标。直到下次通电前都保持这个模式。

  3. now现在Control from your phone用手机操作

    Join the hotspot, sign in, go.连热点、登录，开始。

  4. ~2 minBluetooth wakes (background)蓝牙在后台启动

    Only used for the optional game-controller bridge in USB mode.USB 模式下只用于可选的手柄转接功能。

  1. 0 sHotspot on热点开启

    The flexhub Wi‑Fi starts right away — you can join and sign in now.flexhub Wi‑Fi 立刻开启——现在就可以连接并登录。

  2. ~10–15 sNo computer → wireless mode没有电脑 → 无线模式

    Locked in until the next power-up.直到下次通电前都保持这个模式。

  3. ~2 min“flexhub” appears in Bluetooth蓝牙里出现「flexhub」

    Pair it on the target. Faster: set BOOTSEL single press to “Make Flexhub discoverable (BLE)” and press it.在目标设备上配对。想更快：把 BOOTSEL 单击设为「让 Flexhub 可被发现（蓝牙 HID）」，然后按一下。

  4. paired配对后Control from your phone用手机操作

    Flexhub stops advertising so the hotspot stays smooth.Flexhub 停止蓝牙广播，让热点保持顺畅。

Your phone and the internet手机会断网吗

The flexhub hotspot has no internet. While joined, many phones lose cloud apps; some keep mobile data via “Wi‑Fi assist” / “switch to mobile data”, but that’s up to the phone, not Flexhub. Tip: use a spare phone or tablet as the controller.flexhub 热点本身不能上网。连上后很多手机的联网 App 会暂时断网；部分手机开启「Wi‑Fi 助理」/「自动切换移动数据」后仍可用蜂窝网络，但这取决于手机，不是 Flexhub。小贴士：用闲置手机或平板当遥控器最省心。

“flexhub” isn’t in the list?列表里没有「flexhub」？

Wait until about 2 minutes after power-up and refresh the Bluetooth list. To make it discoverable on demand: open Settings → Device → Physical button · BOOTSEL, set Single press to Make Flexhub discoverable (BLE), save, then press BOOTSEL once — Flexhub stays discoverable for about 90 seconds (LED blinks 3 times).等到通电约 2 分钟后再刷新蓝牙列表。想随时让它可被发现：打开「设置 → 设备 → 实体按键 · BOOTSEL」，把单击设为让 Flexhub 可被发现（蓝牙 HID）并保存，然后按一下 BOOTSEL——约 90 秒内可被发现（指示灯闪 3 下）。

Switch to another device / re-pair换一台设备 / 重新配对

Flexhub connects to one Bluetooth device at a time. On the old device, choose “Forget” / “Remove” for flexhub. Then make Flexhub discoverable again (BOOTSEL as above, or unplug and re-power it and wait ~2 min) and pair the new device. After every firmware update, forget flexhub and pair again — that’s expected.Flexhub 一次只连一台蓝牙设备。先在旧设备上对 flexhub 选择「忽略」/「移除」，再让 Flexhub 重新可被发现（按上面方法按 BOOTSEL，或拔电重新通电后等约 2 分钟），然后用新设备配对。每次升级固件后，都要忽略 flexhub 后重新配对——这是正常的。

Mouse鼠标

  • Slide one finger on the trackpad to move the pointer; a quick tap is a left click; two fingers up/down scroll.单指在触控板上滑动移动指针；轻点一下是左键单击；双指上下滑动是滚动。
  • Buttons: left, middle and right click (hold to drag), scroll up, scroll down.按钮：左键、中键、右键（按住可拖动）、向上滚动、向下滚动。
  • Pencil: while it’s on, sliding holds the left button — handy for selecting text or quick doodles.铅笔：打开后，滑动时会按住左键——适合选文字或随手画两笔。
  • Release all keys (padlock): frees any key or button that seems stuck.松开所有按键（锁形图标）：按键或鼠标似乎卡住时点它。

Creative Pad创意画板

USB mode: the pad maps to the whole screen — touch a spot and the pointer lands on the same spot. Set Canvas ratio (16:9, 4:3, 16:10, 3:2, 9:16, 3:4 or Fill) to match the screen.USB 模式：画板对应整块屏幕——点哪里，指针就到屏幕上同一位置。用画布比例（16:9、4:3、16:10、3:2、9:16、3:4 或填满）对齐目标屏幕。

Wireless mode: Bluetooth mice can’t jump to a spot, so the pad moves the pointer relative to your finger. Use Move scale 1×–8× (default 3×) so strokes travel far enough on big screens.无线模式：蓝牙鼠标无法直接跳到某一点，画板按手指移动量相对移动指针。用移动倍数 1×–8×（默认 3×）让笔画在大屏上走得够远。

  • Shortcut buttons: Undo (Ctrl+Z), Redo (Ctrl+Shift+Z), Eraser (E), Brush (B), Zoom −/+ (Ctrl − / Ctrl +). They use common drawing-app keys; check your app.快捷按钮：撤销（Ctrl+Z）、重做（Ctrl+Shift+Z）、橡皮（E）、画笔（B）、缩放 −/+（Ctrl − / Ctrl +）。采用常见绘图软件快捷键，请以你的软件为准。
  • Pick image puts a reference picture under the pad (it stays in your browser); Bg− / Bg+ resize it, Clear image removes it.选图把参考图放在画板下方（只留在你的浏览器里）；Bg− / Bg+ 缩放，清除图片可移除。
  • 8 pen colours and Clear trail control the guide lines shown on your own screen (not sent to the target). Turn trail lines off in Settings → Device. Rotating your device clears the trail.8 种笔色和清除笔迹用于你自己屏幕上的参考笔迹（不会发送到目标）。可在「设置 → 设备」里关闭笔迹标线。旋转设备会清除笔迹。
  • No pressure levels — great for signatures, PDF notes and light doodles, not a pro pen tablet.没有压感——适合签名、PDF 批注和轻度涂鸦，不是专业数位板。

Keyboard键盘

  • Type a phrase in the box and tap Send. English letters, numbers and standard symbols only — no Chinese or emoji; the result also depends on the target’s keyboard layout and input method.在输入框里打好一句话，点发送。仅支持英文字母、数字和常用半角符号——不支持中文和表情；实际结果还取决于目标设备的键盘布局和输入法。
  • Full keyboard with an Esc PrtSc Ins row, Tab, Caps, Shift, Sym, Enter, Bksp, Del, arrows, Home/End and PgUp/PgDn.完整键盘：顶部是 Esc PrtSc Ins，另有 Tab、Caps、Shift、Sym、Enter、Bksp、Del、方向键、Home/End 和 PgUp/PgDn。
  • Combos: Ctrl Alt Gui stay held until tapped again — tap Ctrl, then C to copy. Gui is the Windows key or ⌘ on a Mac; Alt is ⌥ Option.组合键：Ctrl Alt Gui 点一下保持按住，再点一下松开——先点 Ctrl 再点 C 就是复制。Gui 是 Windows 键，在 Mac 上是 ⌘；Alt 在 Mac 上是 ⌥ Option。

Presenter演示器

  • Previous / Next send Page Up / Page Down — works in PowerPoint, WPS, Keynote and most PDF viewers.上一页 / 下一页发送 Page Up / Page Down——PowerPoint、WPS、Keynote 和大多数 PDF 阅读器都能用。
  • F5 starts the slideshow in PowerPoint / WPS; ⌥⌘P starts Keynote on a Mac; Esc exits.F5 在 PowerPoint / WPS 中开始放映；⌥⌘P 在 Mac 上开始 Keynote 播放；Esc 退出。
  • The presentation app must be the active window on the target.演示软件需要是目标设备上的当前窗口。

Apple TV

USB mode: these tabs simply send the same keys to the computer. For a TV box, use wireless mode.USB 模式：这些标签只会把同样的按键发给电脑。要遥控电视盒子，请用无线模式。

Wireless mode: pair first — Apple TV Settings → Remotes and Devices → Bluetooth → flexhub.无线模式：先配对——Apple TV「设置 → 遥控器与设备 → 蓝牙 → flexhub」。

Swipe on the pad to move, tap to select, or use the arrows. Side buttons: Play/Pause, Home, Back, Esc, Mute, Volume −/+, Power. They are sent as keyboard and media keys, so results can differ from the original remote — check on the TV.在触控区滑动导航，轻点确认，也可用方向键。侧边按钮：播放/暂停、Home、返回、Esc、静音、音量 −/+、电源。它们以键盘和媒体按键发送，效果可能和原装遥控器不同——以电视上的反应为准。

Troubleshooting常见问题

Something not working?哪里不对？

I can’t see the flexhub Wi‑Fi找不到 flexhub Wi‑Fi
  • Give Flexhub a few seconds after power-up, move closer, and refresh the Wi‑Fi list.通电后等几秒，靠近一点，刷新 Wi‑Fi 列表。
  • Still nothing: unplug Flexhub, wait a moment and power it again.还是没有：拔下 Flexhub，稍等后重新通电。
The page won’t open网页打不开
  • Use http://192.168.4.1/ (type the http:// part).使用 http://192.168.4.1/（记得输入 http://）。
  • Wait about 10 seconds after joining; flexhub.local may not work on every device.连上后等约 10 秒；flexhub.local 并非所有设备都支持。
  • Turn off VPN on the controlling phone, and make sure it’s still on flexhub.关闭手机上的 VPN，并确认仍连着 flexhub。
The sign-in password doesn’t work登录密码不对
  • It starts as build.me. If you changed and forgot it, hold BOOTSEL for 5 seconds to reset it (see Settings).初始密码是 build.me。改过又忘了，就按住 BOOTSEL 5 秒恢复（见「设置」）。
USB mode: the computer doesn’t reactUSB 模式：电脑没反应
  • Use a data cable, not a charge-only one, and check both ends.使用数据线而不是充电线，并检查两端。
  • If Flexhub was powered before the cable reached the computer, it may have started in wireless mode (“USB detected late”). Unplug and plug straight into the computer.如果 Flexhub 先通电、后接电脑，可能已进入无线模式（“USB 识别较晚”）。拔下后直接插到电脑上。
Wireless mode: “flexhub” isn’t in the Bluetooth list无线模式：蓝牙列表里没有「flexhub」
  • Wait until about 2 minutes after power-up.等到通电约 2 分钟后。
  • Is it already paired to another device? Forget it there first — Flexhub connects to one at a time.是不是已经连着另一台设备？先在那台上忽略它——Flexhub 一次只连一台。
  • Set BOOTSEL single press to “Make Flexhub discoverable (BLE)” and press it (about 90 s window).把 BOOTSEL 单击设为「让 Flexhub 可被发现（蓝牙 HID）」并按一下（约 90 秒）。
  • Make sure Flexhub is on a charger, not a computer.确认 Flexhub 接的是充电头，而不是电脑。
Bluetooth stopped connecting after an update升级后蓝牙连不上
  • Forget flexhub in the target’s Bluetooth settings, then pair again. This is expected after every firmware update.在目标设备蓝牙设置里忽略 flexhub，再重新配对。每次升级固件后都是这样。
A key or mouse button seems stuck按键或鼠标似乎卡住了
  • Tap Release all keys (padlock on the Mouse tab). Also check Ctrl / Alt / Gui / Shift aren’t still highlighted on the Keyboard tab.点「松开所有按键」（鼠标标签里的锁形图标）。并检查键盘标签里 Ctrl / Alt / Gui / Shift 是否仍在高亮。
Typed text isn’t sent or comes out wrong文字发不出去或变了样
  • Only English letters, numbers and standard symbols can be sent. Switch the target’s input method to English, and check its keyboard layout.只能发送英文字母、数字和常用半角符号。把目标设备输入法切到英文，并检查键盘布局。
